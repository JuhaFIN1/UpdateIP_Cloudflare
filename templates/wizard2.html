Which devices leave through which connection?

What this does: Makes one device always use a chosen internet connection (for example always the second one).
Why it is needed: Useful if you have two connections and want some devices to stay on one of them. You see the change before it is made.
Required: No. Press Next if you do not need it.
Add a rule

Which services arrive on which connection?

What this does: Opens a port on one internet connection and sends it to a device on your network (a port forward).
Why it is needed: Needed only if something at home must be reachable from the internet, for example a web server. You see the change before it is made.
Required: No. Press Next if you do not need it. Opening a port makes that service visible to the internet.
Open a port

Tailscale (optional)

What this does: Connects UpdateIP to your Tailscale account so it can list your Tailscale devices, point a name at one, and help your network reach the tailnet.
Why it is needed: Only if you use Tailscale. UpdateIP reads the device list and changes nothing in Tailscale.
Required: No. Leave it off if you do not use Tailscale.
  1. Open login.tailscale.com/admin/settings/keys, click Generate access token (at most 90 days) and copy it.
  2. Paste it below, press Test, then Save.
Point a name at a Tailscale device
What this does: creates (or changes) a DNS record in Cloudflare that points to the Tailscale address of one of your devices, for example nas.example.com.
Why it is needed: so you can open your device by a normal name while you are on Tailscale. Only devices on your tailnet can reach that address.
Required: No. You see the change before it is made.
Reach your Tailscale devices from your whole network
What this does: adds a route in UniFi so that traffic for Tailscale addresses (100.64.0.0/10) is sent to the device on your network that runs Tailscale.
Why it is needed: so any device at home can reach your Tailscale devices without installing Tailscale on each one. That device must be a Tailscale subnet router (it must be allowed to forward traffic).
Required: No. You see the change before it is made.

Test

What this does: Shows what the DNS update would do right now, record by record. Nothing is changed by the test.
Why it is needed: So you can check that the right records and addresses are used before you finish.
Required: Yes. Press Run test once before Finish.
