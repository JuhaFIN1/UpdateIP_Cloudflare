Setup Wizard

Let's set up UpdateIP

This takes about five minutes. We will:

  1. choose your time zone and the local name of this app,
  2. connect your Cloudflare account,
  3. search your home network for your UniFi gateway and Nginx Proxy Manager,
  4. connect the devices you choose, testing each one before anything is saved.

The search only looks at private addresses on your own network, and only when you press the button. Nothing is changed on your devices. You can run this wizard again later from the menu.

  1. Open dash.cloudflare.com/profile/api-tokens and choose Create Token.
  2. Use the Edit zone DNS template, or add the permissions Zone: DNS: Edit and Zone: Zone: Read.
  3. Copy the token and paste it here. You see it only once.

Find your devices

These are the networks (subnets) this app can see. Remove the ones you do not want to search, or add others, for example a separate server network.

Devices that do not answer on any common port may not show up. If yours is missing, you can type its address in the next step.
